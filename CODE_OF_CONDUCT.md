# AgentLane community guidelines

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of experience, background, identity or choice
of agent tools. Help people contribute and learn.

- Be respectful and patient. Critique ideas and code, and explain your reasoning.
- Assume good intent while taking reports of harm seriously.
- Keep discussions relevant, and respect people's time and boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information or spam project spaces.

These guidelines apply to AgentLane's issues, pull requests, discussions and other spaces
managed by its maintainers. @thetrueshags currently moderates these spaces and may edit or
remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Responses
should reflect the conduct and its impact.

Use GitHub's content-reporting controls to flag abuse or a privacy concern. Do not repeat
sensitive information in a public report. For ordinary disagreements, explain your concern
calmly in the relevant discussion; maintainers make final moderation decisions.
